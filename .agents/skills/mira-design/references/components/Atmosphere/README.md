Atmosphere is a blurred gradient field with film grain, the main carrier of color in Mira.

**You provide:** a `palette`, an optional `height`, and trusted HTML `content` that sits over the field.

- Choose `sunset` for posters and launch pages, `sky` for calm backgrounds, `cobalt` as the ground for glass shapes, and `meadow` for playful sections.
- Use one atmosphere per surface.
- Keep body paragraphs on solid surfaces. Place only short labels and display words on the field, in `on-warm` (sunset, meadow) or `on-cool` (sky, cobalt).
- The field blurs with `blur-field` and the grain layer uses `grain-opacity` with overlay blending.
- Round the tile with `radius-lg`. Override with `radius-xl` for hero tiles.
